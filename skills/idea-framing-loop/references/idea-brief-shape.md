# Idea Brief Shape

Use this as a lightweight idea brief, not a spec or implementation plan. Small ideas do not need a document.

Use existing repo conventions for where to place idea, design, or planning docs. If the right location is unclear, ask before creating an artifact.

## Title

Short descriptive title.

## Status

`draft` / `accepted` / `superseded`

## Problem / Pain

What pain, gap, or opportunity prompted the idea?

## Evidence / Examples

What examples, observations, user requests, incidents, or repeated patterns support it?

## Current Workaround

How is the problem handled today, if at all?

## Target User Or Workflow

Who or what workflow benefits from this idea?

## Goal

What outcome should this idea achieve?

## Non-goals

What should this idea explicitly not solve?

## Smallest Useful Version

What is the smallest version that would create useful learning or value?

## Options Considered

List 2-3 plausible approaches with trade-offs.

## Recommendation

Which approach should be tried first, and why?

## Risks / Ways This Could Become Too Heavy

What could make the idea overbuilt, costly, confusing, or hard to maintain?

## Success Criteria

What would make the idea clearly useful enough to keep or expand?

## Open Questions

What still needs a decision or evidence before planning or implementation?

## Handoff Notes

What should a future planner, implementer, or reviewer know?
